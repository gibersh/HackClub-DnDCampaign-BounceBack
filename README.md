# HackClub-DnDCampaign-BounceBack
Made for DMs with annoying players where, although they don't know it, the only way to level up is making mistakes and bouncing back. Enjoy the Mimics, Gelatinous Cubes, and Kobold-built traps as you force even the most prepared campaigners to obey your will in this incredible DnD experience "Bounce Back."
